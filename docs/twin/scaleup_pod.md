## Inference highlights (INFERENCE)

- one BiDi fiber per lane: `512 × 128 = 65536`
- 16-fiber connectors per mating plane: `65536 / 16 = 4096`
- connectors removed with the fibers: `4 × 4096 = 16384`
- 512 × 128 × 2 duplex fibers: `2 × 65536 = 131072`
- 200G lanes per XPU each way: `25.6 Tbps / 200 Gbps = 128`
- 150 miles of fiber saved: `150 miles × 1609.344 m/mile = 241401.6 m`
- 200 miles of fiber saved: `200 miles × 1609.344 m/mile = 321868.8 m`
- mean length of a removed fiber, HOTI26 figure: `241401.6 m / 65536 = 3.683496 m`
- mean length of a removed fiber, CPX figure: `321868.8 m / 65536 = 4.911328 m`
