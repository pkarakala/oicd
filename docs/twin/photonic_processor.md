## Inference highlights (INFERENCE)

- weight cells per PTC, 128 × 128: `128 × 128 = 16384`
- weight cells in all four PTCs: `4 × 16384 = 65536`
- control die area: `31.4 mm × 25 mm = 785 mm2`
- laser wall power including the TECs, 8 × 1.25 W: `8 × 1.25 W = 10 W`
- V-groove coupling plus vector modulator insertion loss: `1.56 dB + 1.1 dB = 2.66 dB`
- fibre to weight cell, before any splitter excess: `2.66 dB + 1.35 dB = 4.01 dB`
- fibre to weight cell under the laser-mapping hypothesis: `4.01 dB + 0 dB = 4.01 dB`
- multiply-accumulates per cycle, 4 PTCs × 128 × 128: `4 × 16384 = 65536`
- throughput at the 2 GHz clock (intermittent errors): `2 × 65536 × 2 GHz = 262.144 TOPS`
- throughput at the sustained 500 MHz clock: `2 × 65536 × 500 MHz = 65.536 TOPS`
- laser power in fibre, 8 × 200 mW: `8 × 200 mW = 1.6 W`
- bits of the dot-product word under the ADC-range reading: `7 bits + 10 bits + log2(128) − 1 = 23 bits`
- optical power per weight cell (model card §15.5): `1.6 W × 10^(−4.01 dB/10) / 65536 = 9.697059 uW`
- electrical power, PTCs plus control dies (lasers excluded): `12.8 W + 65 W = 77.8 W`
- 78 W electrical plus the laser wall power: `78 W + 10 W = 88 W`
- PTC die area: `24.96 mm × 14 mm = 349.44 mm2`
- electrical power of the four PTCs: `4 × 3.2 W = 12.8 W`
- efficiency at 78 W (lasers excluded): `65.536 TOPS / 78 W = 0.8402051 TOPS/W`
- efficiency including the laser wall power: `65.536 TOPS / 88 W = 0.7447273 TOPS/W`
