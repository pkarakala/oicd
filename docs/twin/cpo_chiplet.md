## Inference highlights (INFERENCE)

- 32T chiplet total (Tx + Rx): `2 × 16 Tbps = 32 Tbps`
- 64T chiplet total (Tx + Rx): `2 × 32 Tbps = 64 Tbps`
- 320 SerDes × payload, one direction: `320 × 100 Gbps = 32 Tbps`
- 320 SerDes × payload, one direction: `320 × 50 Gbps = 16 Tbps`
- fibers at 800 Gb/s each: `32 Tbps / 800 Gbps = 40`
- fibers at 1.6 Tb/s each: `64 Tbps / 1600 Gbps = 40`
- 4 × 64 Tbps per package: `4 × 64 Tbps = 256 Tbps`
- 32 Tbps over 40 pluggable equivalents: `32 Tbps / 40 = 800 Gbps`
