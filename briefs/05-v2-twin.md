- Crosstalk penalty (INFERENCE, labeled): leakage X summed over co-directional channels; penalty = −10·log10(1 − k·X), k = 1 NRZ, k = 3 PAM4 (inner eye is OMA/3), invalid if k·X ≥ 1; also report 10·log10(1 + X) as the alternative form and try to verify it in Agrawal or another open source.

- Thermal: required tuning = laser accuracy (±20 GHz) + fabrication offset (SYNTHETIC ±1 nm) + drift over the temperature swing; with barrel shifting the worst case is one co-directional channel spacing, without it up to one FSR; heater power = tuning/η.

- Aliasing: a ring on channel n with FSR F has images at n ± F/200 GHz; flag images that land on co-directional channels (crosstalk) or counter-directional ones. Outbound light of a counter-directional channel couples to the ring's other circulation and is not dropped (backscatter is the residual path, reported NOT_EVALUATED), but its inbound reflections travel with the signal and are dropped like signal; Phase 6 evaluates that.
