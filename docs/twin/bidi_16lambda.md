## Inference highlights (INFERENCE)

- 8 × 50 Gb/s each way: `8 × 50 Gbps = 400 Gbps`
- 16 × 50 Gb/s per fiber: `16 × 50 Gbps = 800 Gbps`
- photonics plus XSR SerDes: `2.6 pJ/bit + 2 pJ/bit = 4.6 pJ/bit`
- 16 slots of 200 GHz: `16 × 200 GHz = 3200 GHz`
- 16 slots of 200 GHz, in THz: `3200 GHz = 3.2 THz`
- co-directional spacing of two interleaved grids: `2 × 200 GHz = 400 GHz`
- wavelengths each way: `16 / 2 = 8`

## Open tensions and conflicts

- TENSION: `bidi16.check_unique_channel`: 3.2 THz ≤ 1.8 THz (required) fails → TENSION. every channel unique within one FSR: the grid span must not exceed the FSR (model card §13.6)
- CONFLICT: `bidi16.check_kitbidi_lane_rate`: 56 Gbps vs 50 Gbps: gap 12%, tolerance 1% → CONFLICT. 16λ BiDi kit '56 Gbps NRZ' against 50 Gb/s per channel (blog); 8 × 56 is not 400
- CONFLICT: 16λ BiDi kit modulation format and lane rate (Technical Specifications table): 56 Gbps (“Modulation 56 Gbps NRZ”, [vendor_web_kitbidi](https://lightmatter.co/products/passage-evk50/)) against Data rate per wavelength (8 x 50 Gbps = 400 Gbps per direction; 800 Gbps per fiber over 1 km): 50 Gbps (“With each channel operating at 50 Gbps”, [vendor_blog_doubling](https://lightmatter.co/blog/doubling-down-worlds-first-16-%CE%BB-single-fiber-bidirectional-link-for-ai/)). Both are kept.

## Hypotheses

- `H_fsr18`: The 16λ link's rings are the OFC 2025 rings, FSR 1.8 THz = 9 slots of 200 GHz. Agrees with `bidi16.check_bw`, `bidi16.check_per_direction`, `bidi16.check_interleave`, `bidi16.check_mrm_count`, `bidi16.check_energy`, `bidi16.check_fsr_ofc25`; not with `bidi16.check_unique_channel`, `bidi16.check_kitbidi_lane_rate`.
- `H_fsr32`: The 16λ link's rings have an FSR of 3.2 THz, so every channel is unique within one FSR (R ≤ 3.5501 µm at n_g 4.2). Agrees with `bidi16.check_bw`, `bidi16.check_per_direction`, `bidi16.check_interleave`, `bidi16.check_mrm_count`, `bidi16.check_energy`, `bidi16.check_unique_channel`; not with `bidi16.check_fsr_ofc25`, `bidi16.check_kitbidi_lane_rate`.
